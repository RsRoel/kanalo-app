Privacy policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Kanalo is a media player that runs entirely on your device. It has no user accounts, no backend service, and no analytics.

What Kanalo stores

The playlist or portal address you enter, its username and password where one is required, and your own settings — favourites, recently watched channels and reminders. All of it is written to the app's private storage on your device and nowhere else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What Kanalo sends, and to whom

Only to the provider whose address you entered: requests for your channel list, guide data and the streams themselves. Kanalo sends nothing to the developer. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier and no third-party SDK that collects data.

What the developer receives

Nothing. We operate no server that the app talks to, so we hold no data about you at all. If you email us for support, we have whatever you chose to write in that email.

Optional companion server

Kanalo can connect to a self-hosted companion server if you run one yourself. That server is yours; your data stays on your own hardware and does not reach us.

Children

Kanalo is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/RsRoel/kanalo-app/issues.
